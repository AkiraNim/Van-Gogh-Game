### Animations (Player and NPC)

`[signal arg="lock_anim:surprise_up:1"]`
* **Action:** Locks the player's animation (`player_view`) to the specified animation.
* **Parameters:** `animation_name`, `duration_in_seconds`.

`[signal arg="unlock_anim"]`
* **Action:** Frees the `player_view` from its animation lock.

`[signal arg="npc_lock:NPC_Teste:idle_down:1.0"]`
* **Action:** Locks the animation of a specific NPC.
* **Parameters:** `npc_name`, `animation_name`, `duration_in_seconds`.

`[signal arg="npc_unlock:NPC_Teste"]`
* **Action:** Frees the specified NPC from its animation lock.
* **Parameters:** `npc_name`.

`[signal arg="npc_next:NPC_Teste"]`
* **Action:** Makes the NPC advance to its next dialogue timeline.
* **Parameters:** `npc_name`.

`[signal arg="npc_timeline_index:NPC_Teste:2"]`
* **Action:** Forces the NPC to jump to a specific index in its timeline list.
* **Parameters:** `npc_name`, `timeline_index`.

### Inventory and Items

`{last_item_name}`
* **Action:** (Variable) Displays the name of the last "important" item collected.
* **Usage:** `NPC: "Wow, you collected {last_item_name}!"`

`[signal arg="inv_sync"]`
* **Action:** Synchronizes the player's inventory with Dialogic's variables. **(Essential)**
* **Function:** Reads the actual inventory and creates/updates the `has_<item_id>` (bool) and `count_<item_id>` (int) variables for **all** items.
* **Usage:** Must be used **before** any `[IF]` check that involves the inventory.

`[signal arg="remove_item:estrela_amarela:1"]`
* **Action:** Removes a specific quantity of an item from the player's inventory.
* **Parameters:** `item_id`, `quantity` (if omitted, defaults to 1).

### Quest System

`[signal arg="quest_accept:coletar_item:collect"]`
* **Action:** Accepts a new quest and marks it as "accepted".
* **Parameters:** `quest_name`, `quest_type` ("collect" or "talk").

`[signal arg-"quest_set_req:coletar_item:estrela_vermelha:1"]`
* **Action:** Defines the items required to complete a quest.
* **Parameters:** `quest_name`, `required_item_id`, `required_quantity`.

`[signal arg="quest_talk_target:falar_com_ferreiro:Ferreiro"]`
* **Action:** Defines which NPC is the target of a "talk" type quest.
* **Parameters:** `quest_name`, `target_npc_name`.

`[signal arg="quest_add:coletar_item:estrela_vermelha:{count_estrela_vermelha}"]`
* **Action:** Adds progress to a quest (usually used after `inv_sync`).
* **Function:** If the progress meets or exceeds the requirement, the quest is **automatically completed**.
* **Parameters:** `quest_name`, `item_id`, `variable_with_the_quantity`.

`[signal arg="quest_talk_hit:Ferreiro"]`
* **Action:** Signals that the player has spoken to an NPC.
* **Function:** **Automatically completes** any "talk" type quest that has this NPC as its target.
* **Parameters:** `target_npc_name`.

`[signal arg="quest_complete:my_manual_quest"]`
* **Action:** Manually forces a quest to complete.
* **Parameters:** `quest_name`.

### Debug

`[signal arg="quest_dump"]`
* **Action:** Prints the status of all quests (active and completed) to the Godot debug console.
* **Parameters:** None.
